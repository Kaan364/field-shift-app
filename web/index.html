                        Technical details and data sources

                        FieldWise — an open-source decision-support tool built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge.

                        Data: NASA POWER • Map: OpenStreetMap • API

                        How does it work?

                        1. Choose your place. Tap the map or use your location.
                        2. Answer 3 questions. Field size, irrigation and your goal.
                        3. Read your plan. What to plant this year, what comes next, and how your soil, water and money look.

                        NASA POWER data is a regional average and does not describe your whole field. Get a soil test and talk to your local extension service before planting.
